Luna Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

In short: Luna Bloom keeps your cycle data on your phone. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we never share or sell it. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

This policy explains how the Luna Bloom app ("Luna Bloom", "the app", "we") handles your information. It applies to the Luna Bloom app on Android and iOS.

Information stored on your device

When you use Luna Bloom, you may enter period dates, cycle and period lengths, flow, symptoms, mood, notes, basal body temperature (BBT) readings and ovulation (LH) test results. You may also choose app settings such as reminders and a cycle mode.

All of this is stored only in a database on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives your data, and we cannot access, view, recover or delete it for you.

Information we collect

None. Luna Bloom does not collect personal information, health information, device identifiers, location or usage data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Predictions

Period, fertile window and ovulation predictions are calculated entirely on your device from the information you enter. They are estimates only. They are not medical advice or diagnosis, and Luna Bloom must not be used as a method of birth control or to prevent pregnancy.

Purchases

Luna Bloom is free to use, with an optional one-time purchase that unlocks additional cycle modes. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). The app contacts the store only to show the price, complete a purchase and check whether you have already bought it. We never receive your payment details. Google's and Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by their own privacy policies.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device by your phone's operating system. No reminder content is sent over the internet. You can choose "Discreet notifications" so reminders on your lock screen don't mention your cycle.

Backups and exports

You can export your data as a CSV file or create a backup file from Settings. These files are created on your device and saved or shared only where you choose, using your phone's share sheet. If you save a file to a cloud service or send it to someone, that service's or person's handling of it is outside our control. Backup files do not include purchase status.

App permissions

  • Internet access: used only to communicate with Google Play (or the App Store) for purchases.
  • Notifications: to show the reminders you turn on.
  • Run at startup: so scheduled reminders still work after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback and reminders.

Deleting your data

You can remove cycles you've added from Settings. To delete everything, uninstall Luna Bloom; this permanently removes all data stored by the app on your device. Luna Bloom opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so your cycle data is not copied to your Google account. Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, and there is no account to close.

Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your information to anyone, including advertisers, data brokers, employers, insurers or law enforcement. We cannot hand over data we never receive.

Children

Luna Bloom is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and mention the change in the app's release notes. If a future version ever changes how data is handled, we will explain it clearly before the change takes effect.
