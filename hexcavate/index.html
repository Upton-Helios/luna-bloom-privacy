Hexcavate Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

In short: Hexcavate keeps your game progress on your phone. We don't collect any information about you, and there is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

This policy explains how the Hexcavate game ("Hexcavate", "the app", "we") handles your information. It applies to the Hexcavate app on Android and iOS.

Information stored on your device

Hexcavate saves your game on your device so you can pick up where you left off: your current level and board, best depth, best times, Daily Descent results, sound settings, and whether you have bought the full game.

This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it, and we cannot access, recover or delete it for you.

Information we collect

None. Hexcavate does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts or usage data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not use the microphone or camera.

Daily Descent

The Daily Descent board is generated on your device from the date, so every player gets the same board without the app contacting any server. Your result is stored only on your device.

Purchases

Hexcavate is free to play, with an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full game. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). The app contacts the store only to show the price, complete a purchase and check whether you have already bought it. We never receive your payment details. Google's and Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by their own privacy policies.

App permissions

  • Internet access: used only to communicate with Google Play (or the App Store) for purchases.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback during play.
  • Audio settings: to play sound effects and music at the volume you choose.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, uninstall Hexcavate; this permanently removes all game data stored on your device. Hexcavate opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you bought the full game, you can restore it on a new device with "Restore purchase", because Google Play keeps the record of your purchase. Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, and there is no account to close.

Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your information to anyone, including advertisers and data brokers. We cannot hand over data we never receive.

Children

Hexcavate is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and mention the change in the app's release notes. If a future version ever changes how data is handled, we will explain it clearly before the change takes effect.
